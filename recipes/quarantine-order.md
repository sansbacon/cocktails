# Quarantine Order

## glassware
* 16 oz mason jar

## ingredients
* 3 oz rum
* 1 oz lime juice, strained
* 1 oz grapefruit juice, strained
* .5 oz passion fruit syrup
* .5 oz cinnamon syrup
* 5 dashes Angostura Bitters

## equipment
* Jigger
* Mixing tin
* Flash blender

## instructions
1. Add all ingredients to a mixing tin.
1. Fill with 8 oz crushed ice and flash blend 5-10 seconds.
1. Pour into glass, add crushed ice to fill.
1. Garnish and serve.

## recommended brands
* **Rum:** 50/50 of Appleton Signature and Hamilton Jamaican Pot Still Gold
* **Passion fruit syrup:** Liber & Co.
* **Cinnamon syrup:** Liber & Co.

## notes
* Fresh juice is strongly recommended.
* Crushed ice provides the most authentic tiki presentation and dilution.
