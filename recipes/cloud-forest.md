# Cloud Forest

## glassware
* Goblet or double rocks glass

## ingredients
* 1.5 oz vodka (100 proof)
* 1.5 oz coconut mix
* .5 oz lime juice
* 4 drops saline
* 15-18 thai basil leaves
* 1/4 tsp xantham gum
* 170 grams crushed ice

## garnish
* Sprig of Thai basil
  
## equipment
* Jigger
* High-speed blender
  
## instructions
1. Add all ingredients except xantham gum and ice to blender.
1. Dry blend (without ice) until fully combined and slowly add xantham gum.
1. Add ice while continuing to blend

## recommended brands
* **Vodka:** Smirnoff 100 proof
* **Coconut mix:** 50/50 Coco Lopez and 1:1 simple syrup

## notes
* Fresh lime juice is strongly recommended.
* Thai basil can be found at most Asian markets. Regular basil is not a substitute.
